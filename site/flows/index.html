What the engine did, step by step

Each page replays a business scenario that was run through the SmartDelegate engine: who asked for what data, what was allowed, which fields were removed on the way, what was refused and by which rule. The people and the company are fictional. The decisions are the engine's own.

  • The refund that needed a second human

    An AI agent can refund 30.00 on its own. What happens when it asks for 60.00?

    From a real run: engine 0.3.0, 9 decisions, seed 1. Traffic view: 6,408 decisions by 200 simulated agents

  • She left at 4 pm. Her agents stopped at 4:00:01

    An employee leaves in the middle of a task. What happens to the AI agents still working with her access?

    From a real run: engine 0.3.0, 7 decisions, seed 1. Traffic view: 6,408 decisions by 200 simulated agents

  • Stolen token, still on a leash

    A capability token ends up with the wrong agent. What can the holder do with it?

    From a real run: engine 0.3.0, 7 decisions, seed 1. Traffic view: 6,408 decisions by 200 simulated agents

Every page works offline and has a text transcript. Three views: the story, the traffic of a larger simulated run, and the audit trail as a chain of hashes. Build 0.3.0. No independent security review yet. What the product does not do is in docs/LIMITATIONS.md; each page names the limit that applies to it. Run the scenarios yourself with the small harness in harness/. Open source, Apache-2.0.
