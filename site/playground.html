SmartDelegate playground

A human lends an agent one role they hold, for one task. Pick who asks and what they ask for, and see what the token would carry. Then hand part of it to a sub-agent.

This page illustrates the delegation rules only. Real decisions (policy, field masking, budgets, audit) come from the smartdelegate engine: pip install sf-smartdelegate, then sf-smartdelegate --demo.
