# Maintainers

| Who | Role |
|---|---|
| SmartTasks Lab | Maintainers: the owners of the SmartTasksOrg GitHub organisation |

The maintainers are the owners of the GitHub organisation
[SmartTasksOrg](https://github.com/SmartTasksOrg). They decide what is
released and when, and how a vulnerability report is handled
([SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)).

Releases are published only by `.github/workflows/release.yml` through
trusted publishing, after a second maintainer approves the `pypi`
environment. No maintainer uploads from a laptop.
